Interactive globe

Click any country to fly in, select it, and load its full profile. Switch between GDP and growth map layers, toggle day/night, or rotate automatically.

News & events

A live feed of top stories filtered by country or topic, with automated sentiment tags, plus a rolling feed of global events from the last 72 hours.

Markets

Benchmark stock indices per country, spot prices for gold, silver and oil, FX rates across major currencies, and commodity quotes — refreshed every minute.

Weather

Hourly and 7-day forecasts anywhere on earth, with air quality, UV index, visibility and wind — plus an optional live precipitation radar overlay on the globe.

Economy

GDP, inflation, unemployment and debt ratios from World Bank Open Data, with historical trend charts for every economy.

AI assistant

Ask anything about any country — security risks, growth drivers, climate outlooks — and get a sourced, concise answer in seconds.
